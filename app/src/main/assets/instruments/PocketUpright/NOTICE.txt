PianoPocket adaptation: 12 sampled keys across A0–C8, three velocity layers,
44.1 kHz stereo PCM16. Original layer gains (23 / 10 / 2 dB) followed by
one shared headroom adjustment, tails limited to 12 seconds and faded over
the final 150 ms. Original round-robin variants are not included.
WAVs are losslessly gzip-packed in the APK and unpacked on installation.
SFZ mapping and preparation script: MIT, see project LICENSE.
Source and converted sample SHA-256 hashes are in manifest.json.
No Helsinki / Pianobook samples are included.
